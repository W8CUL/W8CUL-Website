Membership Benefits

Shack Access

Use our fully-equipped radio station with HF, VHF, and UHF capabilities in ESB 529.

License Training

Free study sessions and VE testing to help you earn your amateur radio license.

Community

Connect with fellow radio enthusiasts, from beginners to experienced operators.

Projects

Participate in hands-on projects like balloon launches, APRS trackers, and more.

Contests

Compete in amateur radio contests and represent WVU in Field Day events.

Career Skills

Develop technical skills and networking connections valued by employers.

"Joining W8CUL was the best decision I made at WVU. I came in knowing nothing about radio, and now I have my General license and have made contacts with stations around the world. The club is incredibly welcoming to newcomers."

Aidan K. (N3AMK) - Club President, Electrical Engineering
