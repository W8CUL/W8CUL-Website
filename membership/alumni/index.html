Alumni

The W8CUL Alumni Network connects generations of amateur radio operators who developed their skills at West Virginia University. Our alumni have gone on to remarkable careers in aerospace, technology, research, and beyond.

Featured Alumni Spotlight

K8JT

Dr. James Thompson, K8JT

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory | Senior Systems Engineer

Class of 1998 PhD Electrical Engineering

Dr. Thompson credits his time at W8CUL with sparking his passion for space communications. "The hands-on experience I gained working with satellite communications at the club directly prepared me for my career at NASA. The problem-solving skills and technical foundation I developed at W8CUL have been invaluable."

Dr. Thompson has worked on communications systems for Mars rovers and the Voyager mission. He regularly mentors current club members and has helped establish internship connections with JPL.
